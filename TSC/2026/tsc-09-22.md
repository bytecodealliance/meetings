### Agenda
The TSC reviewed the agenda for the meeting and a final agenda was agreed upon.

### Topic #1
The TSC reviewed current governance topics as identified across issues and pull requests in the Alliance governance and project repositories, recognition of project maintainers, consideration of projects for Hosted or Core Project status, Special Interest Group activities, nominations for Recognized Contributor, communications received by the TSC, ongoing standards efforts within the W3C Community Group, topics of broad scope in the Alliance's Zulip community, and a recap of the most recent Alliance board meeting. Proper follow-up actions will be taken by reviewers on the requests and issues discussed, including scheduling meetings needed to advance active topics and providing an update at the next Alliance board meeting. The proposal to create SIG-Component-Model was approved and will be published in the Alliance governance repository. A final decision was made regarding the transfer of the cap-std project to private maintainers outside the Alliance, and steps identified to carry that out. Work to develop the next generation of StarlingMonkey is sufficiently far enough along to allow preliminary discussions of how and when to onboard elements into formal Bytecode Alliance Hosted Projects including StarlingMonkey itself. 

### Topic #2
Possible locations and dates for the next Alliance-hosted "Plumbers Summit" event were discussed. Various options for hosting locations were identified, which TSC members will investigate in anticipation of further discussion at next week's meeting.  

### Adjournment
There being no other business to come before the meeting, it was adjourned at approximately 11:10am PT.
